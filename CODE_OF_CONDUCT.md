# Code of Conduct

Vinyl follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming, be respectful, assume good intent, and give constructive feedback. Harassment, insults, and personal or political attacks aren't tolerated in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else in the project.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits and contributions that don't follow this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behaviour they consider harmful.

To report a problem, open a [private security advisory](../../security/advisories/new) (it's private to maintainers) or contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile.
